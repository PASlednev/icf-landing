How to play Ice Fishing

Every round starts with a betting window. You can back the Leaf segments, which pay out instantly, or the fish segments (Lil' Blues, Big Oranges and Huge Reds) that lead to a bonus round, and an All Bonuses bet covers all three. The 53-segment money wheel is then spun, and random multipliers of up to 10x may be added to segments before it stops. If it lands on a fish segment, a bonus catch mini-game reveals a random multiplier: Lil' Blues pays up to 100x, Big Oranges up to 200x and Huge Reds up to 500x, building to a top win of 5000x once wheel multipliers are applied. Outcomes are random and independent, so there is no pattern or 'right moment' you can predict.
